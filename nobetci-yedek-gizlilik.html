nobetci-yedek – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

nobetci-yedek is a private, single-user backup tool. It is operated by its owner (osmanb0) solely to back up data from the owner's own server to the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • The app requests only the drive.file scope. It can see and manage only the files and folders it has created itself (a folder named nobetci-yedek). It cannot read, list or change any other file in Google Drive.
  • The files it uploads are backups of the owner's own server (a database and configuration files). No personal data of other people is collected or processed.

How data is used and shared

  • Backups are stored only in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Google user data is not transferred anywhere other than between the owner's server and the owner's Google Drive.

Retention and deletion

Backups older than 30 days are deleted automatically. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the nobetci-yedek folder removes all backups.

nobetci-yedek – Gizlilik Politikası

nobetci-yedek, sahibinin (osmanb0) kendi sunucusundaki verileri yine kendi Google Drive hesabına yedeklemek için kullandığı, tek kullanıcılı ve herkese açık olmayan bir araçtır.

  • Yalnızca drive.file iznini kullanır: Drive'da sadece kendi oluşturduğu nobetci-yedek klasörünü görebilir; diğer dosyalara erişemez.
  • Yüklenen dosyalar sahibin kendi sunucusunun yedekleridir; başka kişilere ait veri işlenmez.
  • Veriler üçüncü taraflarla paylaşılmaz, satılmaz, reklam veya analiz için kullanılmaz.
  • 30 günden eski yedekler otomatik silinir. Erişim izni myaccount.google.com/permissions adresinden istenildiği zaman kaldırılabilir.
